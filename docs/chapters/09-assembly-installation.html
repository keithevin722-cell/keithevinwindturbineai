Chapter 9: Assembly & Installation

Suggested assembly order

  1. Assemble frame and verify shaft-bearing alignment.
  2. Mount generator and coupler; check smooth hand rotation.
  3. Install rotor arms and blades; torque fasteners with threadlocker where specified.
  4. Route electrical cabling with strain relief and weatherproof glands.
  5. Connect controller, dump load, and battery with proper fusing.
  6. Perform no-load spin test before tower raise.

Tower/foundation and guy wires

  • Use rated anchors and tension guy wires evenly.
  • Re-check plumb after first high-wind event.
  • Keep clear radius around spinning rotor; post warnings.
Work-at-height warning: Never install in high wind. Use helper crew, lifting plan, and fall-protection systems.
