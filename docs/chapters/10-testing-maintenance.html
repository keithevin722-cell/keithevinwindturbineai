Chapter 10: Testing, Commissioning & Maintenance

Commissioning tests

  1. Continuity/polarity check: Verify all major conductors and protective devices.
  2. Cut-in test: Record wind speed and RPM where charging begins.
  3. Load test: Observe battery charging current and controller diversion behavior.
  4. Thermal check: Confirm rectifier, controller, and cabling temperatures stay in safe range.

Maintenance schedule

  • Monthly: visual check, guy wire tension, unusual noise/vibration.
  • Quarterly: torque checks, corrosion inspection, cable insulation checks.
  • Annually: bearing condition/lubrication service per bearing spec, full electrical retest.

Troubleshooting quick guide

  • Low output: check wind resource, blade balance, bearing drag, loose electrical joints.
  • No charging: inspect PMA AC output, rectifier polarity, fuse continuity, controller settings.
  • Overheating: increase wire gauge, improve ventilation, verify dump load operation.
Storm protocol: If your controller supports safe braking/diversion, enable storm mode. Do not approach the turbine during severe weather.
