Chapter 7: Generator/Alternator Selection & Mounting

PMA vs axial-flux custom builds

Beginners should usually start with a commercially available PMA designed for small wind. Custom axial-flux alternators can perform well but add design/manufacturing complexity.

RPM and voltage matching

  • Check cut-in RPM at your intended battery voltage (12 V or 24 V).
  • Use pulley/gearing only if necessary; direct-drive is simpler and reliable.
  • Confirm current rating with expected peak output and gust conditions.

Mounting checklist

  1. Use rigid generator mount plate with slotted holes for alignment.
  2. Ensure coupler concentricity to reduce vibration.
  3. Add weather protection and drip loops on cable exits.
Tip: For this book's baseline build, a 24 V PMA in the 400 W-1 kW range is a practical target.
