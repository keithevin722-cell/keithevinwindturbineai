Chapter 11: Appendix - Glossary of Terms

VAWT
Vertical-axis wind turbine; rotor spins around a vertical shaft.
HAWT
Horizontal-axis wind turbine; common propeller-style turbine.
Swept Area (A)
Area of wind intercepted by the rotor; often D × H for VAWTs.
TSR (Tip-Speed Ratio)
Blade tip speed divided by incoming wind speed.
Cut-in Speed
Wind speed where turbine starts producing useful charge power.
PMA
Permanent magnet alternator/generator commonly used in small wind systems.
Rectifier
Converts AC from PMA into DC for batteries/controllers.
Dump Load
Resistive load that absorbs excess power to control turbine speed and battery overcharge.
Charge Controller
Regulates charging to protect battery bank and manage diversion load.
Ampacity
Maximum safe current a cable can carry continuously.
