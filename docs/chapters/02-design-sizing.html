Chapter 2: Design & Sizing

Pick a realistic first build

Recommended first design target: 1.5 m rotor diameter × 1.8 m rotor height, 24 V battery system, and a 400 W-1 kW PMA.

Swept area

For VAWTs, swept area is typically A = D × H. For 1.5 m × 1.8 m, A = 2.7 m².

Power estimate basics

Use the wind power relation:

Pwind = 0.5 × ρ × A × V³ where ρ ≈ 1.225 kg/m³.

Electrical output is lower: Pout = Pwind × Cp × η. Small DIY VAWTs often have Cp around 0.15-0.30 and drivetrain/electrical efficiency maybe 0.6-0.8.

Tip-speed ratio (TSR)

TSR = blade tip speed / wind speed. Small H-rotors commonly run around TSR 2-4. Match blade shape and generator load so RPM rises smoothly without stall or overspeed.

Site and wind resource

  • Measure/estimate average wind speed at intended hub height.
  • Avoid severe turbulence behind roofs/trees where possible.
  • Use higher placement and clear exposure for better output.
Code and permitting: Tower placement, height, setbacks, and electrical interconnection may require permits and inspections.
