Chapter 5: Blade & Rotor Construction

Material choices

For first builds, common options are formed aluminum sheet, PVC sections, or fiberglass composite. Aluminum usually gives the best durability-to-weight ratio for a long-term outdoor machine.

Template and matching

  1. Create one master blade template in cardboard or thin MDF.
  2. Transfer and cut all blades from the same template for consistency.
  3. Deburr every edge and round sharp corners.
  4. Drill mounting holes using a jig to keep spacing identical.

Balancing

  • Static balance each blade; remove small material where needed.
  • Assemble rotor and check for preferred heavy spot.
  • Correct with tiny counterweights or light grinding only.
Blade safety: Never test spin with spectators nearby. Use a temporary guard zone and tether if possible during initial checks.
