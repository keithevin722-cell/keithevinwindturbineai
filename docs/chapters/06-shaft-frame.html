Chapter 6: Shaft, Bearings & Frame/Tower Construction

Shaft alignment first

Frame geometry must keep top and bottom bearings collinear. Misalignment causes heat, noise, and premature bearing failure.

Build sequence

  1. Fabricate base frame and vertical uprights.
  2. Install lower pillow block, then upper support bracket.
  3. Dry-fit shaft through both bearings and verify free rotation.
  4. Add rotor supports and check runout before final torque.

Tower options

Short mast with guy wires is common for hobby projects. Keep center of gravity low during test phase and move to full height after successful commissioning.

Structural warning: Use conservative design factors for gust loading and vibration. If unsure, get a qualified structural review.
