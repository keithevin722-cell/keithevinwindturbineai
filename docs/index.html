The DIY VAWT Book

A practical, beginner-friendly guide to designing, sourcing, building, wiring, and maintaining a small hobbyist vertical-axis wind turbine (VAWT).

Safety and legal disclaimer: This book is educational only and is not a substitute for licensed engineering, electrical, or structural advice. Spinning machinery, batteries, and electrical systems can cause severe injury, fire, or death. Always follow local electrical/building codes, permitting requirements, and manufacturer instructions.

Who this book is for

If you are a hobbyist who wants to build a small VAWT (about 1.0-2.0 m rotor diameter, 400 W-1 kW class generator), this manual gives a full end-to-end path from concept to commissioning.

Book baseline design target

  • Rotor type: small H-rotor or Savonius hybrid
  • Rotor size example: 1.5 m diameter × 1.8 m height
  • Generator class: 24 V PMA, roughly 400 W to 1 kW
  • Use case: battery charging and light off-grid loads
